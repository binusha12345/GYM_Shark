Gymshark is a British fitness apparel and accessories brand, manufacturer and online retailer headquartered in Solihull, UK.

Founded in 2012 by Ben Francis and Lewis Morgan while they were still in university, Gymshark has grown from a screen-printing operation in a garage, into one of the fastest growing and most recognisable brands in fitness.
